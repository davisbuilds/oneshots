You can only see what the room has heard.

Ring sends one of three tones into the dark. Each colored wave obeys the same small wave equation: it spreads, bends around corners, interferes with itself, and returns from walls. The phosphor afterglow remembers where sound has been.

Wall draws reflective stone. Erase opens it again. Listen moves the small listening point; turn on sound to hear the field passing through it. Try ringing two colors from opposite sides of The Fold.

Keys 1–3 choose a chamber, Q/W/E choose a tone, R rings its center, Space pauses, M toggles sound, C clears the afterglow, and S keeps an atlas.

This is an expressive acoustic sketch, not an engineering simulator. Its two-dimensional rooms and softened boundaries favor beautiful, legible echoes. Everything runs inside this file.
