A field guide to a perfect crime

Recruit your past.

Every attempt begins at the same moment. You have 60 seconds, up to five echoes, and one very valuable hourglass.

  1. Hold the west station. Walk onto its gold circle and leave an echo. The museum resets; your echo repeats the trip and then stays there.
  2. Hold the east station. Leave a second echo on the blue circle. When both stations are occupied, the vault’s laser barrier opens.
  3. Make the final run. Walk into the vault, take the hourglass, and bring it to the green entry circle before time runs out.

Guards see echoes and investigate whistles. Use your past as a distraction. Only the live you can be caught. Marble walls and displays block their vision. Running is faster, but nearby guards can hear it.

Feeling ambitious? The First Moon in the west wing and Entanglement in the east wing can also be stolen. Press E nearby. Bring all three treasures home for grand larceny; extra echoes can buy you cover.
