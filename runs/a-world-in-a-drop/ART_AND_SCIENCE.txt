A WORLD IN A DROP
Production and interpretation notes

A glint becomes a lens; a lens becomes a world; a world becomes a gaze.

This is a poetic, science-inspired film. It is not scientific microscopy,
a scale-accurate journey, or a claim that miniature oceans inhabit human eyes.
Its connective tissue is visual: curved water, branching veins, porous silica,
folded boundaries, radial fibres, and the dark aperture of a pupil.

THE JOURNEY (the shots overlap during 1.5-second dissolves)
00:00–00:10   The Atlantic. Rain approaches a wind-driven sea.
00:08.5–00:19.5   One Drop. The camera accompanies a drop to a leaf.
00:18–00:30   Silica Garden. Perforated forms drift in a suspended world.
00:28.5–00:38.5   Inner Tides. Folded membranes become a living passage.
00:37–00:47   The Weave. The passage resolves into a field of fine strands.
00:45.5–00:58.5   The Witness. Those strands resolve into an iris.
00:58.5–01:02   Closing title; the sound of the sea subsides.

WHAT COMES FROM NATURE
- The ocean uses Blender's spectral Ocean modifier: an approximate wind-driven
  surface with choppiness, generated foam, and a second scale of wind texture.
  This is a visual wave model, not a weather forecast or validated fluid study.
- Surface tension, a leaf's branching venation, and wet specular highlights
  informed the drop and leaf. The water material uses an IOR of 1.333.
- Diatoms really do build intricate silica frustules. The open pores and
  radial organization are inspired by these structures. The film's model is
  not a reconstruction of an identified species.
- Biological membranes fold and separate environments; iris tissue has a
  complex radial organization. These broad observations motivated the forms.

WHAT IS INVENTED
- The apparent continuous descent crosses imaginary thresholds. Object units
  are local artistic units; there is no common physical scale across scenes.
- The falling drop, impact crown, settling motion, and drift are keyframed.
  They are not fluid, molecular, or Brownian simulations.
- The diatoms' glowing central beads, their scale relationships, and their
  choreography are invented. The glow does not represent a measured nucleus.
- The membrane tunnel and gold/teal filament field are abstract constructions,
  not organelle reconstructions, atomic orbitals, or quantum visualizations.
- The palette, emissive edges, lighting, and depth of field are expressive.
  They should not be interpreted as natural microscopic colors.
- The procedural eye simplifies anatomy. Its layered fibres and crypts are
  an artistic approximation, not a scan of a person's iris. A transparent,
  curved corneal shell reflects an off-camera card carrying the opening ocean.
  The reflection is ray-traced, but the staging and anatomy are simplified for
  the film and are not a validated optical model of the human eye.
- The ending's nested world is a metaphor about observation and resemblance.

SOUND
All audio is original synthesis. Filtered noise forms the sea, rain, breath,
and distant thunder. Slowly detuned sine partials and inharmonic glass tones
carry an understated D-centered harmonic field. No sampled recordings,
commercial music, external sound libraries, or generative audio services were
used. The score's source is source/score.py; the original stereo WAV is included.

VISUAL PROVENANCE
All scene geometry, materials, lighting, and animation were built in code and
rendered conventionally in Blender Cycles. No image-generation or video-
generation models were used. The ocean reflection is rendered from this very
project. FFmpeg supplies temporal interpolation, dissolves, a vignette, titles,
encoding, and the final audio mix.

ITERATION
The preserved rough animatic is a 2 fps timing study. It intentionally records
an earlier ocean model and the camera roll that review caught near the ending.
Subsequent revisions darkened the lighting, refined the leaf's veins, added
actual holes to the silica surfaces, replaced the ocean with a wave spectrum,
aligned the circular transitions, and stabilized the final gaze. Following
review, the eye was rebuilt with irregular stroma, quieter crypts, overlapping
lids, longer curved lashes, and a reflected ocean on a transparent cornea. Earlier
images in studies/ are development material, not alternate final deliverables.
A small corneal pole shading defect was repaired with localized Blender renders
composited into the early eye frames and selected eye still. The editable
geometry incorporates that correction; fresh full renders need no such patch.

TOOLS
Blender 4.3.2 official Linux distribution, Cycles CPU rendering, OpenImageDenoise,
Python, NumPy, SciPy, Pillow for review sheets, and FFmpeg 7.1.5.
The official bpy 4.3.0 Python module was also installed in a Python 3.11
environment; production renders use the official Blender background CLI.

Titles use DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans. DejaVu is a freely licensed font family.
The native edit packs its title font, along with the original audio and ocean
reflection. See LICENSES.txt for the font license and tool references.
