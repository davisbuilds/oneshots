A WORLD IN A DROP — provenance and licenses

Original project material
All scene geometry, procedural materials, lighting, animation, synthesis code,
score and sound design were created for this project. No third-party stock
photography, footage, music, sample library, or generated image/video asset is
included. The ocean texture in the eye is an original render of scene 01.

Fonts
The closing title uses DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans. Both fonts are bundled in
src/renderers/fonts/ (staged as source/fonts/ during a build) and packed into the native edit. The Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license and notices are reproduced
in src/renderers/fonts/LICENSE.txt (staged as source/fonts/LICENSE.txt). Font information: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/
