# Progress log

Concise record of the build, decisions and verification, kept by the agent
during the run. Times are UTC, 2026-09-26 to 27. During the run the section
headings carried rough session-relative estimates. Those were wrong: the whole
modeling and look-dev phase took under two hours, not five. The times below
are reconstructed from tool-call timestamps in the session transcript and from
commit times, and are accurate to a few minutes. Snapshots referenced here are
in `snapshots/`, numbered in the order they were made. Paths in sections 1–9
are as they were during the run: `artemis-ii/render/` is now `output/` and
`docs/` is now the run root (see section 10).

## 1. Environment (19:17–19:35) — `snapshots/001`
- Container: 4 × Xeon @ 2.1 GHz, 15 GB RAM, no GPU. No Blender installed; download.blender.org, nasa.gov, esa.int, Wikipedia are blocked by the environment's egress policy.
- Blender 5.0.1 installed as the `bpy` module from PyPI (Python 3.11 venv). Cycles CPU, Workbench and (via Mesa llvmpipe) EEVEE all render headless; ffmpeg via apt.
- Fonts: IBM Plex (SIL OFL) from the npm registry, converted WOFF2 → TTF with fontTools.

## 2. Research (19:19–19:32)
- Only web-search summaries were reachable; every fact is logged with its source in `docs/REFERENCE.md` (now `REFERENCE.md`) and tagged verified / derived / estimated.
- Key verified facts: vehicle 322 ft; core 212 ft × 27.6 ft; booster 177 ft × 12 ft; ICPS 45 ft × 16.7 ft; LVSA ~30 ft, 27.6 → 16.8 ft; OSA 5 ft × 18 ft; Orion stack 67 ft; CM 11 ft × 16.5 ft; RS-25 start at T-6.36 s, 120 ms apart, order 3-1-4-2; umbilical levels on ML-1; Artemis II worm rotated ~45° forward on the boosters; fiducials on all booster segments; liftoff 1 Apr 2026 18:35 EDT.
- Sun position computed (NOAA algorithm) for LC-39B at liftoff: elevation 13.5°, azimuth 268°.
- Station layout derived so that the 212 ft core, 30 ft LVSA, 67 ft Orion stack and 322 ft total close consistently; the fit implies the RL10B-2 nozzle extension is stowed on the pad.

## 3. Modeling (19:35–20:00) — `snapshots/002–004`
- Geometry is generated by Python (`src/lib/*.py`): lathe profiles, sweeps, ribbed shells, booleans. 96 named vehicle objects in collections CoreStage / RS25_Engines / SRB_North / SRB_South / UpperStack / Orion; origins at interface rings.
- Decals drawn procedurally (`src/make_decals.py`): worm logotype approximation, US flag, simplified "esa" plate.
- First checks: full-stack proportions, base, upper stack and booster/intertank close-ups rendered and reviewed.

## 4. Studio and choreography (19:58–20:10) — `snapshots/005–006`
- Act II reordered to echo the real build order: core stage joins top-down (forward join first), RS-25s installed last in ignition order, boosters stacked bottom-up, LVSA → ICPS → OSA → Orion.
- Workbench animatic of the studio acts reviewed via contact sheets; fixed an opener that was too tight, a hero framing that clipped the LAS tip, and a too-narrow exploded layout.
- Look-dev in Cycles; fixed flat MLI, a cloudy floor, and a component shot where an opened SAJ panel blocked the ESM.

## 5. Performance (20:12–20:30) — `snapshots/007`
- 1080p/24 spp close-ups measured at 60–70 s/frame. Profiling showed: fixed ~7 s (sync + denoise), multi-octave noise shaders expensive, emissive light bars sampled as lights, floor reflections costly.
- Fixes: noise detail capped at 2 octaves, light bars made reflection-only, floor simplified, per-shot light hiding, bounces reduced. Final: 1600×900 at 12 spp (studio) / 10 spp (pad) + OIDN, upscaled to 1920×1080 in the edit.
- Found that persistent data retains the first sample count within a session (early "fast" benchmark numbers were invalid; re-measured).

## 6. Pad and launch (20:32–21:00) — `snapshots/008–013`
- Pad 39B/ML-1 built to the verified dimensions; tower east of the vehicle (assumption), flame trench north.
- Launch physics integrated from thrust/mass; tower clear ≈ T+7 s.
- Effects: deterministic geometry-node particles (steam, smoke trail, water jets, igniter sparks), additive emission plumes (RS-25 Mach diamonds; brighter turbulent booster plumes), glow lights, mist-pass aerial perspective, restrained fog glow.
- Five look-dev rounds: fixed double sun (sky disc + lamp), front-lit cameras (moved to side light), smoke engulfing the liftoff camera (exhaust biased north/east, deflector direction), blob-like water, rock-like clouds.

## 7. Sound (21:02–21:06) — `snapshots/014`
- Original procedural sound design (`src/sound.py`); inspected with a loudness/spectrogram report (`src/audio_report.py`). Rebalanced so the launch is the dynamic peak (≈ -9 dB RMS) with a quiet anticipation dip before ignition.

## 8. Animatic, verification and final renders (21:00 – 09:57 next day) — `snapshots/015–035`
- Full-film Workbench animatic (studio every 2nd frame, pad every 4th) assembled with typography and sound into `film/ARTEMIS_II_animatic_540p.mp4` and reviewed via contact sheets.
- Animatic findings fixed before final rendering: pad steam emitted inside the ML footprint rose through the deck and engulfed the RS-25 start close-up (moved to the ML perimeter, venting away from camera sightlines); booster label leader crossed the core (re-anchored); long label clipped at frame edge (columns moved inward).
- Automated assembly-collision check (`src/check_collisions.py`): world AABB overlap of 51 parts per frame vs. their assembled (nested) overlap. Found Orion parked where the exploded ICPS/OSA pass through (22–33 s); fixed by parking Orion higher until the upper stack closes. Re-check: 0 flagged pairs over all 1,345 studio frames.
- Flicker check: two renders of the same RS-25 macro frame with different noise seeds differ by 2.2 levels mean (p99 10) after OIDN; seed is fixed across frames, so frame-to-frame flicker is below that.
- Label legibility checked on composited frames (26 px condensed caps at 1080p).
- Final renders (from about 21:15; restarted at 22:03 after the collision fix): `SC_Studio` and `SC_Pad` rendered in parallel (2 threads each), resumable PNG sequences.
- (22:40–23:17, `snapshots/021–027`) Launch-pad water reworked after reviewing the first final S14 frames: the rainbird jets rendered as glassy sheets, then (as puffs) as cloud masses in front of the engines. Now thin droplet streaks along a fire-hose arc (10–12.5 m/s at 30°, 9–12 m throw); core-opening steam made smaller and barely emissive; RS-25 start glow lowered so the exhaust opening no longer clips. The 121 pad frames rendered with the old water were moved to `render/superseded/` and re-rendered.
- The auto-mode safety check blocked deleting those 121 frames and then overwriting the `.blend`. The agent stopped at 23:20 and asked; the human approved at 23:44 (human turn 2). The frames were moved rather than deleted. While the session sat idle, the container restarted and killed both renders; they resumed from the last complete frame (sequences are resumable by design).

S11 camera eased back (366 m, target +2 m) and the shot re-rendered; label tracks re-exported. Overlay type got a soft shadow for legibility over bright sky; the countdown now shows the next whole second (T–00:00:01 until liftoff).

## 9. Edit, delivery (10:57–11:49) — `snapshots/036–038`
- Final assembly: 2,137 frames at 1920×1080/24 fps (the 24-frame dissolve blends both scenes), H.264 CRF 18 + AAC, 89.04 s, 64 MB. ffprobe verified; contact sheet of every 3rd second reviewed; end title and mission clock legible over sky.
- Loudness (EBU R128): −19.9 LUFS integrated, −0.5 dBTP, LRA 9.9 LU.
- Stills rendered natively at 3840×2160 (40 spp, adaptive 0.02, OIDN high). The launch still first rendered the wrong moment (stills script did not switch the active scene before `frame_set`); fixed and re-rendered, and all stills switched to 8-bit RGB PNG (16-bit masters were ~40 MB each).
- Superseded frames re-rendered after review: 547 (old water 121, old smoke 113, cropped LAS 300, box scrub 13).
- The first launch still (frame 1812) rendered the pad before ignition: `stills.py` set the frame on a scene that was not the active one, so the evaluation did not follow. Caught by looking at the still, not by a check; that image was not kept.
- Delivered at 11:49 on the branch as committed files: the film (64 MB), stills, `.blend` and animatic.
