# Design studies

**Optical plate.** This study isolated the real simulation field and three enlarged living specimens so membrane and fin geometry could be checked without the station interface. The instrument later switched to a silhouette-aware fit to keep appendages inside the plate. The optical tint includes both a cosmetic illumination field and a sampled nutrient field. [Specimen study](optical-plates.webp)

**Behavior controller.** The early model decided whether to hunt from hand-tuned conditions. It could sustain births after adjusting costs, but pursuit was largely a consequence of the authored rule. The shipped model evolves signed steering and actuator coefficients. A probe compares opposite controller alleles in the same sensor configuration. [Controller tests](../../tests/engine.test.cjs)

**Ancestry display.** An initial design kept descendants under a permanent founder label. That hides substantial within-family divergence. The implemented atlas retains founder families and adds nested branches when inherited genetic distance crosses the stated threshold. Individual parent links always remain available independently of this classification.

**Replay.** Recording live images would have made the time lens inexpensive to seek and expensive to retain. Reconstructing from seed and timed exposures allows the inspector, plots and camera to operate at a historical tick. The cost is seek time proportional to elapsed ticks. Paired cultures use the same reconstruction to establish the moment of intervention.
