Small acts. Living consequences.

Carve a channel through the soil. Water it, or send a passing shower. Plant seeds on the ground. Build a little dam.

Blue drops fall and spread. Soil absorbs nearby water and shares moisture with its neighbors. Plants draw from the soil at their roots; growth slows when that water is gone. Dark soil is damp soil.

The pale stone at the bottom cannot be carved. Earth you build stays where you put it, like clay. This is a small cellular ecology, not a scientific watershed model.

Keys 1–4 choose tools. Arrow keys move the circular cursor. Hold Space to use it. R brings rain, P pauses, and [ ] change tool width. New earth begins again.
